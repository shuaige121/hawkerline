Showcase deployment

坡晓馒 PO XIAO MAN

坡晓馒 PO XIAO MAN is a Singapore bun (包点) shop. Hawkerline was extracted from the production system that runs its online ordering.

Visit poxiaoman.com

What runs on the system

  • Online ordering storefront, owner back office, POS and kitchen, all on the system Hawkerline was extracted from.
  • Cloudflare Workers and D1.
  • Live Stripe payments.
  • A bilingual Chinese and English menu, with combos, family boxes and drinks.
  • The storefront is at poxiaoman.com; the back office, POS and kitchen use their own subdomains of the same Worker.

The storefront

Screenshots of the public storefront at poxiaoman.com.

Why it matters for Hawkerline

Hawkerline is a source-available restaurant ordering, kitchen display, menu and POS system that an AI agent can operate, built for Singapore food businesses and running on one Cloudflare Worker and one D1 database. The showcase is the reference: Hawkerline is not a prototype written for a demo, but the code that was extracted from a system serving a real shop.
