License FAQ

Is Hawkerline free?

It is free for a food and beverage business to run for its own outlets, if the business together with its affiliates had annual revenue of not more than S$10,000,000 in the previous financial year. Personal, evaluation, educational and non-commercial use is also allowed.

Can my IT contractor set it up for me?

Yes. An eligible business may install, modify and self-host Hawkerline, and may have an IT contractor set it up for the business's own use.

What can a vendor or agency not do?

Without a separate commercial license, system vendors, POS or SaaS providers, agencies and integrators may not sell, resell, sublicense, host it as a service for other businesses, or bundle it into a commercial product.

What happens above S$10,000,000 in annual revenue?

A business above that threshold needs a commercial license. Contact the licensor by opening an issue in the GitHub repository.

Is Hawkerline open source?

It is source-available, not OSI open source. You can read and modify the source, within the terms of the Hawkerline Community License.

How is annual revenue counted?

Revenue counts the business together with its affiliates, for the previous financial year. The threshold is S$10,000,000 or less.

Can an IT contractor charge me for setup?

Yes. A contractor may be paid to set Hawkerline up for one eligible merchant. The contractor may not sell the software or run one deployment for several businesses.

What if my business grows past S$10,000,000?

After the threshold is exceeded you have 90 days to obtain a commercial license. Open an issue in the GitHub repository to contact the licensor.

No. Hawkerline is provided as-is, without warranty.
